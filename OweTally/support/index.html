Support

We cannot restore or recover your data.

OweTally does not collect your app data. Your records are stored locally on your device, and Moenish has no server copy, backup, or access to them.

Restoring your records requires an existing usable backup that you control. If your device and all usable backups are lost, we cannot retrieve or reconstruct your records. We do not offer a backup repair or data-recovery service.

For help, bug reports, or privacy questions, email moenish.dev@gmail.com. Include your app version, device model, operating-system version, and what happened. Remove personal names and financial details from screenshots where possible. Please do not send your ledger, receipt files, or backups. Support can answer questions about using the app, but cannot restore your records for you.

Back up and restore your records yourself

In OweTally, open Settings → Data management → Export backup and choose a destination. The backup includes records, settings, and attachments. It is not encrypted, so store it securely. Keep a copy somewhere you can still access if your phone is lost or stops working. Moenish never receives a copy through this feature.

To restore, choose Restore backup, select the file, review the preview, and confirm. Restoring replaces the existing data. Export your current records first if you need to keep them. Only information contained in the selected backup can be restored; later changes are not included. A backup that is missing, damaged, or incompatible may not be usable. Your operating system may also offer device-backup restoration under its own settings; Moenish cannot access or restore those backups.

Delete your data

OweTally has no online account or developer-hosted ledger to delete.

  • Individual records: use the available delete actions in the app. Some linked records must be removed before a person can be deleted. Archiving only hides a record; it does not erase it.
  • All local data on Android: open your device’s Settings → Apps → OweTally → Storage, then select Clear storage / Clear data. Menu names vary by device. Clearing only the cache does not remove the ledger.
  • All local data on iOS: use Settings → General → iPhone Storage → OweTally → Delete App. Offloading keeps app data.
  • Copies outside the app: separately delete exported backups, shared files, and copies in device or cloud backups. A later restore can bring those records back.

Deletion is permanent for the affected local copy. Moenish cannot recover or remotely erase your ledger. To request deletion of emails you previously sent to support, contact moenish.dev@gmail.com.
