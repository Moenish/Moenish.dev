Your own record

We do not collect any data from the app.

All OweTally records, receipts, attachments, and settings are stored locally on your device. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, or developer-operated cloud sync. The app never sends your ledger to Moenish. Other people do not need to install the app.

We cannot restore or recover your data. We have no access to your records and keep no backup. Export backups regularly and keep a usable copy somewhere safe. If your data is lost, you must restore it yourself from a backup you control.

Your device's backup services and the apps you choose for sharing or saving files may handle copies. See how your data is handled.

OweTally records expenses and repayments; it does not send money or connect to bank accounts.

Questions about using OweTally?

Support can help explain the app's features. It cannot retrieve lost records, repair backups, or restore data for you.
